Frequently Asked

What is Push to Start Ventures?

P2SV is a health and wellness venture studio based in Los Angeles. It designs, builds, launches, and operates its own portfolio companies directly — a builder-operator model, not a fund and not an accelerator.

What does P2SV build for a venture to reach commercial readiness?

Each venture gets a 0 to 1 build: product and technical infrastructure, plus the brand, messaging, and direct-to-consumer go-to-market needed to launch and sell — all under one roof, with no handoffs to outside agencies or dev shops.

Is P2SV a venture capital fund or an accelerator?

No. P2SV owns and operates its ventures directly rather than investing limited partner capital into founders it doesn't control. It evaluates investment, co-build, and strategic partnership opportunities selectively, venture by venture.

Who does P2SV partner with?

Independent med spa and wellness operators seeking technology and client-experience partners; angel investors and early-stage VCs evaluating health and wellness technology; and entrepreneurs seeking a studio partner to co-build a venture from concept to launch.
